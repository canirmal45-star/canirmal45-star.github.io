Privacy Policy

Toilet Trouble (the game at canirmal45-star.github.io and the Android app "Toilet Trouble", package com.salvo.toilettrouble), made by SALVO.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version

Toilet Trouble does not collect, store or share any personal information. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

What the game saves

The game saves two small things only on your own device:

  • your best score
  • whether you turned the sound on or off

These are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not need any special permissions on your phone.

Hosting and Chrome

The game's files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

On Android, the app runs inside Google Chrome. Chrome's own data handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
